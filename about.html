Who I am

I am René Mourambou Diyema, a Quantitative Analyst focused on quantitative models for financial markets—from assumptions and calibration to prices, hedges and risk measures.

I develop, calibrate and validate models used in derivative pricing, volatility and risk, drawing on stochastic calculus, Monte Carlo methods and statistical inference. My training spans applied mathematics, machine learning and mathematical finance (ESIEE Paris, ENS Paris-Saclay, Paris-Saclay University, Gustave Eiffel University).

I have research and industry experience in model estimation and validation, forecast stability and recalibration, and numerical modeling of complex dynamics. I am fluent in both French and English.
